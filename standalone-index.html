Institutional Profile

About Salama Legacy Centre

A beacon of academic inquiry, ethical practice, and socio-economic transformation.

Salama Legacy Centre was established under the visionary leadership of Dr. Muthoka J.K, PhD, to provide an academic and practical nexus where empirical research directly informs community empowerment and institutional effectiveness. Located strategically in Salama Town, along the major Mombasa-Nairobi corridor in Makueni County, the Centre serves as a catalyst for regional advancement.

Our multidisciplinary portfolio spans baseline and impact evaluations, demographic and socio-economic field investigations, specialized statistical training, institutional governance advisory, and innovative community interventions. We partner with academic institutions, county governments, international development agencies, non-governmental organisations, and private enterprises to tackle complex socio-economic challenges.

Anchored by our motto, "Service with Dignity is Our Hallmark," the Centre is committed to conducting rigorous, unbiased research that respects the dignity of research participants while equipping leaders and communities with transformative knowledge.

Methodological Discipline

Our Research Workflow

A structured, rigorous 6-step methodological lifecycle ensuring data integrity from concept to dissemination.

01

Conceptualisation & Inception

Refining the problem statement, literature review, theoretical framework, research questions, and stakeholder objectives.

02

Methodological Design & Tool Protocol

Designing sampling strategies, survey protocols, interview guides, ethical review submissions, and digital survey configuration.

03

Fieldwork & Empirical Data Collection

Deploying trained field researchers, conducting rigorous quality checks, GPS verification, and real-time supervisory data audits.

04

Analysis & Synthesis

Executing advanced statistical modeling, econometric analysis, qualitative thematic coding, and cross-tabulations.

05

Validation & Policy-Grade Reporting

Convening stakeholder validation workshops, synthesizing executive summaries, technical reports, and actionable policy briefs.

06

Translation into Action & Monitoring

Supporting dissemination, institutional integration of findings, and longitudinal monitoring of community or programmatic impact.
